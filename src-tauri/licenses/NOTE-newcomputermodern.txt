New Computer Modern — GPL with a font exception
===============================================

The New Computer Modern font family is one of Typst's default fonts, embedded
in the InkyCap binary through the `typst-assets` crate (`fonts` feature).

It is distributed under the GNU General Public License (v3) together with a
*font exception*, the same arrangement TeX/LaTeX fonts conventionally use. The
exception clarifies that documents which merely embed or include the fonts (for
example, a PDF InkyCap exports) are NOT considered derivative works of the
fonts and are therefore not subject to the GPL. Modifying and redistributing
the font files themselves remains governed by the GPL.

The full GPLv3 text is in `GPL-3.0-or-later.txt`. The font's own License.txt
(carrying the exact exception wording) ships with the font package.

Project: https://ctan.org/pkg/newcomputermodern
