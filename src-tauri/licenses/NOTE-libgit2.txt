libgit2 — GPLv2 with a linking exception
=========================================

InkyCap statically vendors libgit2 (via the `git2` crate's `vendored-libgit2`
feature) for its notebox git-collaboration transport.

libgit2 is licensed under the GNU General Public License version 2, *with a
special linking exception* that explicitly permits combining and distributing
libgit2 with code under terms of your choosing — i.e. linking it into a program
that is not itself GPL. This exception is what makes embedding libgit2 in
InkyCap permissible.

The exact wording from libgit2's COPYING file:

  In addition to the permissions in the GNU General Public License, the authors
  give you unlimited permission to link the compiled version of this library
  into combinations with other programs, and to distribute those combinations
  without any restriction coming from the use of this file. (The General Public
  License restrictions do apply in other respects; for example, they cover
  modification of the file, and distribution when not linked into a combined
  executable.)

The full GPLv2 text is in `GPL-2.0-or-later.txt`.
