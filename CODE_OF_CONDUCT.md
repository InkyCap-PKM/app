# Code of Conduct

Briefly, we ask that you be respectful and treat others with courtesy. Understand the context of your use of the application and participation in the community: namely that it was created and is maintained by a person or people that do so as a labour-of-love and enjoy sharing.

___

**Effective Date:** 9 June 2026

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 3.0.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make the InkyCap community welcoming, safe, and equitable for all.

We are committed to fostering an environment that respects and promotes the dignity, rights, and contributions of all individuals, regardless of characteristics including race, ethnicity, caste, colour, age, physical characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language, philosophy or religion, national or social origin, socio-economic position, level of education, or other status. The same privileges of participation are extended to everyone who participates in good faith and in accordance with this Code of Conduct.

## Encouraged Behaviours

While acknowledging differences in social norms, we all strive to meet our community's expectations for positive behaviour. We also understand that our words and actions may be interpreted differently than we intend based on culture, background, or native language.

With these considerations in mind, we agree to behave mindfully toward each other and act in ways that centre our shared values, including:

1.  Respecting the **purpose of our community**: using and advancing InkyCap's personal knowledge management objectives together.
2.  Engaging **kindly and honestly** with others.
3.  Respecting **different viewpoints** and experiences.
4.  **Taking responsibility** for our actions and contributions.
5.  Gracefully giving and accepting **constructive feedback**.
6.  Committing to **repairing harm** when it occurs.
7.  Behaving in other ways that promote and sustain the **well-being of our community**.

## Restricted Behaviours

We agree to restrict the following behaviours in our community. Instances, threats, and promotion of these behaviours are violations of this Code of Conduct.

1.  **Harassment.** Violating explicitly expressed boundaries or engaging in unnecessary personal attention after any clear request to stop.
2.  **Character attacks.** Making insulting, demeaning, or pejorative comments directed at a community member or group of people.
3.  **Stereotyping or discrimination.** Characterizing anyone's personality or behaviour on the basis of immutable identities or traits.
4.  **Sexualization.** Behaving in a way that would generally be considered inappropriately intimate in the context of the  community.
5.  **Violating confidentiality.** Sharing or acting on someone's personal or private information without their permission.
6.  **Endangerment.** Causing, encouraging, or threatening violence or other harm toward any person or group.
7.  Behaving in other ways that **threaten the well-being** of our community.

### Other Restrictions

1.  **Misleading identity.** Impersonating someone else for any reason, or pretending to be someone else to evade enforcement actions (including creating alternate accounts).
2.  **Intentional disruption.** Deliberately stalling, frustrating, or otherwise behaving in bad faith.
3.  **Promotional materials.** Sharing marketing or other commercial content in commentary or community spaces without significant contextual justification.

## Reporting an Issue

Tensions can occur between community members even when they are trying their best. Not every conflict represents a Code of Conduct violation, and this Code of Conduct reinforces encouraged behaviours and norms that can help avoid conflicts and minimize harm.

When an incident does occur, it is important to report it promptly. To report a possible violation, please contact jc@inkycap.org.

Reports are taken seriously and will be investigated as promptly as possible. All reports will be handled with transparency, discretion, and the privacy of the reporter will be respected to the extent possible.

## Addressing and Repairing Harm

If an investigation finds that this Code of Conduct has been violated, the following enforcement measures may be used, based on the severity of the violation and its impact on the individuals involved and the community as a whole. Depending on the severity, earlier steps may be skipped.

1.  **Warning**
    
    -   _Event:_ A violation involving a single incident or series of minor incidents.
    -   _Consequence:_ A private, written warning.
    -   _Repair:_ Acknowledgement of responsibility and a commitment to avoid repeating the behaviour.
2.  **Temporarily Limited Activities**
    
    -   _Event:_ A repeated violation after a warning, or a first instance of a more serious violation.
    -   _Consequence:_ A time-limited restriction on certain activities (e.g., discussion forum participation).
    -   _Repair:_ Using the cooldown period to reflect on the behaviour and its impact, apologizing, or other options.
3.  **Temporary Suspension**
    
    -   _Event:_ A pattern of repeated violations, or a single serious violation.
    -   _Consequence:_ Temporary suspension of forum access, temporary ban from other avenues of participation in InkyCap development or communities with conditions for return.
    -   _Repair:_ Respecting the suspension period and meeting the specified conditions.
4.  **Permanent Ban**
    
    -   _Event:_ A pattern of violations that other measures have failed to resolve, or a violation so serious that continued membership is incompatible with community safety.
    -   _Consequence:_ Permanent removal of access to the forum or other InkyCap communities and development participation.

This enforcement framework is a guideline. The operator and any appointed moderators retain discretion to take actions they deem appropriate in the best interests of the community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all interactions with respect to developing InkyCap, participating in its online forum, or otherwise participating in the InkyCap community. It also applies if representing the InkyCap community in external spaces.
